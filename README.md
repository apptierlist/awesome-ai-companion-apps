# Awesome AI Companion Apps [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> iPhone apps in which a language model plays a persistent character you talk to, as a companion, a friend, a partner or a story partner.

AI companions differ from general chat assistants in one way: the character is the product. It keeps a personality, carries the relationship from one session to the next, and in many apps develops over time. This list covers apps on the App Store.

## Companions

General-purpose companions: one ongoing relationship with a character that remembers you.

- [Animates](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/animates-life-companions/id6758621319) - Companions you talk to by voice or text, each with its own personality and daily life.
- [Crushie](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/crushie-al-chat-companion/id6741790631) - Companion you design yourself, then chat with in an open-ended conversation.
- [Kindroid](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kindroid-your-personal-ai/id6451038161) - Companion you build from scratch, with text and voice chat, generated selfies and optional web access.
- [Otherhalf](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/otherhalf-ai/id6470370773) - 3D companions you can talk to out loud, with animated reactions.
- [Replika](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/replika-ai-companion-chat/id1158555867) - Long-running companion that talks by text, voice call and video, and keeps a diary of what it learns about you.

## Friends and wellbeing

Platonic companions built around support, routines and everyday conversation.

- [Friends: Pengu, Bao and Mellow](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/friends-pengu-bao-mellow/id6462927800) - Virtual pets with AI personalities that nudge routines, self-care and focus, shared with a friend.
- [Livia](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/livia-emotional-best-friends/id6739728455) - Emotional support friend with journaling and self-care tools built into the chat.
- [MeetAI](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/meetai-chat-with-ai-friend/id6447675508) - Create virtual friends with their own backstories and chat with them.
- [Soul.App](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/soul-app-the-ai-friend/id6755092667) - Customizable friend you can text, call and play small games with.
- [Tolan](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tolan-your-friendly-guide/id6477549878) - Friendly alien guide you talk with by voice, share photos with, and build a small planet alongside.

## How apps are selected

An app is listed when it ranks in the top two tiers (S or A) of its use case on [AppTierList](https://apptierlist.com/) and has at least 20 App Store ratings. AppTierList computes the tiers from public App Store signals: star rating, number of ratings, search position in five countries and update recency, with nothing paid or hand-picked. See [how the ranking works](https://apptierlist.com/how-we-rank/). Within each section, apps are in alphabetical order.
