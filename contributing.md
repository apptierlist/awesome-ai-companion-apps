# Contributing

Suggestions are welcome through a pull request or an issue.

An app is added when it meets the selection rule in the readme: it ranks S or A in its use case on [AppTierList](https://apptierlist.com/) and has at least 20 App Store ratings. Apps that drop below the rule are removed at the next monthly review.

When you open a pull request:

- Add one entry in the section that fits, in alphabetical order.
- Use the format `- [App Name](App Store link) - Description.`
- Keep the description to one sentence that says what the app does. No superlatives, no pricing claims.
- Link to the App Store listing without tracking or referral parameters.
